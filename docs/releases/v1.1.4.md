VoiceFlow v1.1.4 fixes development-folder disk growth and installer folder selection.

### Fixed

- Release packaging removes temporary test, build, and publish folders on success or failure.
- The installer detects source-code folders and chooses a separate App subfolder instead.
- Interactive and silent installs cannot place application files directly into a source checkout.
- Developers can retain temporary files deliberately with Build-Release.ps1 -KeepBuildFiles.

The installed application files use about 195 MB, including .NET and the uninstaller.
Downloadable model weights, optional backends, and Bark's decoded cache are stored separately in your Windows profile and require additional disk space. Their behavior is unchanged.

Close VoiceFlow from its tray before upgrading. Use the installer, or extract the entire portable ZIP and launch VoiceFlow.exe. Both include .NET. The installer is unsigned.

Validation: all 96 application tests passed; installer compilation, portable ZIP integrity, checksums, and automatic staging cleanup were checked.
